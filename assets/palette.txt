# Colours (Atari colour bytes: hue * 16 + luminance, in hex). Used by the game (via tools/build_assets.py) and by
# the level editor, so what you see in the editor is what the game shows.
#
# The playfield has four colours. Every row of tiles gets its own set, switched by a display-list interrupt:
#
#   pixel 1  the hue of the row: hues[(row + level index) mod 8], so the colour steps along the list from one
#            row to the next (a ladder shades from orange to magenta) and every level starts somewhere else
#   pixel 2  steel grey
#   pixel 3  white (ice blue in an alt tile)
#
# Row 0 (the score row), the title screen and the game over screen are not banded: they use "hud" for pixel 1.
#
# Mr. Bubble has three colours (assets/sprites.txt). The first two are entries of their own:
#
#   player  his body, hardware Player 0 ('#' in sprites.txt)
#   eyes    his eyes, hardware Player 3 ('e')
#
# The third ('o': the pupils and the grin) is drawn with the four missiles as a fifth sprite, and the hardware gives
# the missiles the colour of playfield colour 3. So his THIRD COLOUR IS THE "ice" ENTRY BELOW: change ice and the ice
# tiles, the exit door's blue and his pupils/grin all change together. (There is no separate entry for it.)

hues       1E 2C 4C 5C 7C 9C AC CC
grey       0A
white      0E
ice        8A        # also Mr. Bubble's third colour (see above)
hud        2A
background 00
player     4E
eyes       0E
